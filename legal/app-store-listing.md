## Description (4000 chars max)

```
Deadpoint tells you what to train today — and only today. No weekly
schedule to plan around, no spreadsheet, no guessing whether yesterday's
session means today should be a rest day. Open the app, see one
recommended session, do it.

HOW IT WORKS

Answer a short quiz — what you climb, how experienced you are, what
equipment you've got, anything worth flagging — and Deadpoint assigns you
a real training plan built around it. From there, the app tracks your
recent training and recommends each day's session based on genuine
recovery logic, not a fixed calendar: skip a few days for a work trip or
an injury niggle, and the plan picks up from where you actually are, not
where a spreadsheet says you should be.

WHAT'S IN THE APP

• One clear recommendation a day — no overwhelm, detail only when you
  tap for it
• A real periodized plan: base, strength, power and performance phases,
  with automatic deload weeks
• Built-in rest and interval timers, so you're not reaching for a
  separate stopwatch mid-session
• Weight tracking per exercise, with targets that adjust as you progress
• A home-screen widget showing your next few days at a glance
• Works for both bouldering and sport climbing, beginner through advanced

WHO IT'S FOR

Anyone who wants real structure without becoming a spreadsheet exercise
of their own — from someone newer to structured training through to
climbers who've hit a plateau pure mileage won't break.

Deadpoint is not a medical device and doesn't replace professional
coaching or medical advice — see the in-app Terms for details. Consult a
doctor before starting any new training program, particularly if you
have an existing injury.

Deadpoint Standard includes a 7-day free trial, then renews at
£0.99/month. Cancel any time in Settings.
```
1777 chars — well under the 4000 limit, room to expand once real screenshots/positioning are locked.
