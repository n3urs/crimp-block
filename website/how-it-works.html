The rules underneath

Recovery is the limiter, not the calendar.

The engine never asks what week of a programme you're on. It asks what your last seven days actually contained, and then applies gates in a fixed order.

Fingers get their own ceiling

Pull is systemically hard but it's a rest day for fingers. Counting both against one combined cap strangled the rotation, so finger-loading days are capped separately, because connective tissue adapts slower than muscle, and that's the limit that matters for injury.

Three on, one off: earned, not scheduled

Each session returns as soon as the tissue it loads has recovered. Three hard days on the trot and it stops you: nothing productive happens on day four.

Deloads and layoffs tighten it further

Week four of every block pulls both ceilings down. Come back from ten days off and you get a two-session taper at 85% of your loads, because cold tissue meeting a near-max weight is how people get hurt.

Change your mind later, without starting over

Got a pull-up bar for Christmas? Gym membership lapsed? Trip booked for April, and you want to pivot from bouldering to sport for it? Change the answer in Settings and the plan updates from that day forward. Your logged history, your block and your week count all carry on exactly where they were. Nothing resets.
